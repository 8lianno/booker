# Non-Fiction Dossier Format v1 — Output Contract

This contract defines the output structure for **Non-Fiction** book dossiers produced by `booker`. It combines executive-level conceptual clarity with deep chapter breakdowns, argument chains, mental model matrices, and active recall banks.

## Purpose

A reader who has not read the non-fiction book will understand:
1. The core thesis, problem system, and authorial worldview.
2. The overarching argument architecture and mental models.
3. Full terminology definitions and conceptual distinctions.
4. A chapter-by-chapter narrative breakdown that connects ideas across chapters.
5. Main argument chains, evidence ledgers, and empirical support.
6. Load-bearing vs. secondary content.
7. Reader misunderstandings and critical limits of the theory.
8. Practical synthesis, decision protocols, and action steps.
9. Active recall memory hooks and self-test question bank.

---

## The 10 Mandatory Sections

### 1. Executive Orientation & Big-Picture Thesis
- **Core Thesis Statement**: The single central claim of the book.
- **Problem System**: The problem or status quo misconception the author opposes.
- **Intellectual Context**: Where this book fits in its field or industry.

### 2. Worldview & Architectural System
- **Foundational Axioms**: Unstated or explicit starting assumptions.
- **System Architecture**: High-level map of how the book's parts fit together.

### 3. Full Terminology & Mental Model Matrix
- Standardized entries for every major term, mental model, or framework:
  `| Term / Model | Definition & Meaning | Role in System | Key Example | Common Misunderstanding | Anchor |`

### 4. Chapter-by-Chapter Narrative Journey
- For each chapter:
  - **Chapter Thesis**: Central point of the chapter.
  - **Why it Matters & Progression**: How it builds on previous chapters.
  - **Key Arguments & Evidence**: Load-bearing points and quoted evidence.
  - **Core Retain Idea**: The single takeaway to remember.

### 5. Main Argument Chain & Evidence Ledger
- Step-by-step logical sequence (`A1` Assumptions → `P1` Premises → `M1` Mechanisms → `C1` Conclusions).
- **Evidence Matrix**:
  `| Claim Supported | Evidence Item | Evidence Type | Weight / Credibility | Anchor |`

### 6. Load-Bearing vs. Secondary Material
- **Indispensable**: Core concepts without which the book collapses.
- **Supporting / Illustrative**: Helpful examples and case studies.
- **Secondary / Rhetorical**: Anecdotes or tangential points.

### 7. Reader Misunderstandings & Critical Limits
- 5+ Misreading vs. Correction pairs.
- Boundary conditions, edge cases, and where the theory fails or does not apply.

### 8. Practical Application & Decision Protocols
- Actionable protocols, decision trees, or tactical habits derived from the text.

### 9. Memory Hooks & Compressed Flash Cards
- 20 must-remember ideas.
- 15 compressed memory hooks.

### 10. Self-Test Question & Recall Bank
- Chapter-by-chapter recall questions with comprehensive answers anchored to source text.
